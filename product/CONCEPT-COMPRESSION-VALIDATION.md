# Concept Compression & Validation Gate

## Rule

**EXPLORE WIDELY → THEN COMPRESS**

Compression happens only after divergent ideation and Creative Depth. It must not retroactively shrink the North Star.

## Current concept

**COLLIDER — Semantic CI for Agentic Software Development**

Current wedge:
> A short cross-boundary specification allows parallel implementation workstreams to make incompatible but locally plausible choices. COLLIDER separates agent drift from a true missing decision, asks the minimum clarification, compiles it into executable artifacts, and guards future work.

## Before → After contract

### Before

- 3 workstreams locally green.
- 30 local tests pass.
- 2 cross-boundary integration conflicts remain.
- One mismatch is source-explicit AGENT_DRIFT.
- One mismatch is source-silent SPEC_GAP / UNKNOWN.

### Product transformation

- Retrieve source authority.
- Refuse to invent missing authority.
- Ask one bounded human decision.
- Compile decision into spec, code, regression contract, and memory.
- Verify affected work.
- Guard future work against resolved-canon regression.

### After

- 0 integration conflicts.
- SEMANTICALLY_READY.
- Future incompatible change is blocked.
- Exact verified state is restored.

## Real-User Break Test protocol

A participant who did not build COLLIDER should receive only:

1. the public URL;
2. one sentence: “Three workstreams are locally green but disagree at integration. Use COLLIDER to understand and resolve what happened.”

Do not coach the participant through the controls.

Observe:

- Do they understand the problem in ~10 seconds?
- Do they distinguish AGENT_DRIFT from SPEC_GAP?
- Do they understand why KEEP UNKNOWN is a success state?
- Can they reach the human decision without explanation?
- Do they understand 2→0?
- Do they discover or understand the guard second act?
- Can they explain the product back in one sentence?

Record failure points before polishing the final video.
